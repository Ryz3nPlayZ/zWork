- Only claim expenses dated in the month you're reporting on.
- Personal purchases are never reimbursed. Leave out anything marked personal.
- Claim each expense once. A photo and an emailed copy of the same receipt are one expense.
- Foreign currency: use the exchange rate printed on the receipt. If none is printed, use 1 EUR = 1.08 USD.
